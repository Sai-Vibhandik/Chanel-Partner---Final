1. Introduction

Welcome to Channel Partner Portal

The Channel Partner Portal is a comprehensive platform designed for Real Estate Development Companies to manage their channel partners, properties, site visits, commissions, and legal agreements efficiently.

What This Portal Offers

  • Partner Management - Onboard, verify, and manage channel partners with ease
  • Property Listings - Showcase properties to partners for client visits
  • Visit Scheduling - Book and manage site visits, office visits, and virtual tours
  • Commission Tracking - Track and process partner commissions transparently
  • Agreement Management - Create, assign, and collect digital signatures on agreements
  • Real-time Communication - Chat and notification system for seamless communication

Who Can Use This Portal

User Type Description
Real Estate Companies Property developers who want to manage their channel partner network
Channel Partners Real estate agents/agencies who bring clients to properties
Company Staff Employees managing partners, properties, finances, etc.

2. Getting Started

2.1 Registration

Before you can access the portal, you need to register your account. Choose the appropriate registration path:

Option A: Register as a Company

Use this option if you are a Real Estate Development Company wanting to use the platform.

  1. Navigate to Registration Page - Go to the portal URL and click on "Register Company" button on the login page.
  2. Fill Company Details - Enter the following information:
    • Company Name
    • Company Email (This will be the admin email)
    • Phone Number
    • Password (Minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character)
    • Company Website (Optional)
    • Company Address
  3. Verify Your Email - After submitting, you will receive a verification email. Click the verification link to activate your account.
  4. Complete Profile - After verification, log in and complete your company profile by adding logo and additional details.
Note: The email used during company registration becomes the Company Super Admin account. This account has full access to all company features.

Option B: Register as a Partner

Use this option if you are a Real Estate Agent or Agency wanting to partner with companies.

  1. Navigate to Registration Page - Go to the portal URL and click on "Register Partner" button on the login page.
  2. Fill Personal Details - Enter the following information:
    • Full Name
    • Email Address
    • Phone Number
    • Password
  3. Verify Your Email - Click the verification link sent to your email to activate your account.
  4. Complete KYC (Know Your Customer) - Upload required KYC documents to verify your identity. This may include:
    • Government ID (PAN Card / Aadhaar Card)
    • Address Proof
    • Business Registration (if applicable)
  5. Apply to Companies - After registration, browse and apply to partner with real estate companies on the platform.

2.2 Login

  1. Go to Login Page - Navigate to the portal URL. You will see the login form.
  2. Enter Credentials - Enter your registered email address and password.
  3. Access Your Dashboard - After successful login, you will be redirected to your role-specific dashboard.
Your Role Dashboard You Will See
Company Super Admin Company Dashboard
Partner Manager Partner Manager Dashboard
Property Manager Property Manager Dashboard
Finance Manager Finance Manager Dashboard
Channel Partner Partner Dashboard

2.3 Password Recovery

If you forget your password, follow these steps:

  1. Click "Forgot Password" - On the login page, click the "Forgot Password" link below the login form.
  2. Enter Your Email - Enter the email address associated with your account.
  3. Check Your Email - You will receive a password reset link. Click the link in the email.
  4. Create New Password - Enter your new password and confirm it. The link expires after a limited time.
Security Tip: Choose a strong password with at least 8 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Never share your password with anyone.

4. Company Admin Guide

As a Company Super Admin, you have full control over your company's presence on the platform.

4.1 Dashboard Overview

The Company Admin Dashboard displays:

  • Total Partners: Count of active partners
  • Active Properties: Number of listed properties
  • Pending Visits: Visits awaiting approval
  • Pending Commissions: Commissions awaiting approval
  • Recent Activity: Latest actions on the platform

4.2 Managing Company Profile

To Update Company Profile:

  1. Click on Settings in the sidebar
  2. Update company details (name, description, address, contact info)
  3. Upload company logo
  4. Click Save Changes

4.3 Managing Team Members

To Add Team Members:

  1. Go to Team from the sidebar
  2. Click Add Team Member button
  3. Enter member details:
    • Full Name
    • Email Address
    • Phone Number
    • Role (Partner Manager, Property Manager, Finance Manager, or Viewer)
  4. Click Send Invitation
Note: The invited member will receive an email with login instructions. They must verify their email before accessing the platform.

To Edit Team Member:

  1. Go to Team page
  2. Find the member and click Edit
  3. Update role or details
  4. Click Save

To Deactivate Team Member:

  1. Go to Team page
  2. Find the member and click Deactivate

4.4 Managing Partners

To View Partner Applications:

  1. Go to Partners from the sidebar
  2. Filter by status: Pending, Active, Rejected, Suspended

To Approve/Reject Partner Application:

  1. Click on a pending partner application
  2. Review KYC documents uploaded by the partner
  3. Click Approve or Reject
  4. If approving, set the commission rate for this partner

To Assign Properties to Partner:

  1. Go to Partners and click on partner name
  2. In partner details, find Assigned Properties section
  3. Click Edit and select properties to assign
  4. Click Save

4.5 Managing Office Locations

Office locations are used for scheduling office visits with partners.

To Add Office Location:

  1. Go to Office Management from sidebar
  2. Click Add Office
  3. Enter office details:
    • Office Name
    • Address (Street, City, State, Pincode)
    • Contact Number
    • Working Hours (Start Time, End Time)
    • Capacity (Max concurrent visits)
  4. Click Save

4.6 Managing Agreements

To Create Agreement Template:

  1. Go to Agreements from sidebar
  2. Click Create Agreement
  3. Enter agreement details:
    • Agreement Title
    • Agreement Type (Partnership, NDA, Commission, etc.)
    • Content (Use the text editor)
  4. Click Save as Draft or Publish

To Assign Agreement to Partners:

  1. Open the agreement
  2. Click Assign to Partners
  3. Select partners from the list
  4. Click Send for Signature

5. Partner Manager Guide

As a Partner Manager, you are responsible for managing channel partners and their visit requests.

5.1 Dashboard Overview

  • Total Partners: Count of partners under management
  • Pending Applications: New partner applications to review
  • Today's Visits: Visits scheduled for today
  • Pending Approvals: Visit requests awaiting action

5.2 Managing Partner Applications

To Review Partner Application:

  1. Go to Partners page
  2. Click on Pending tab or filter by "Pending" status
  3. Click on partner name to view details
  4. Review KYC documents:
    • PAN Card
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Business Registration (if applicable)
    • Other documents
  5. Click Approve or Reject with reason

5.3 Managing Visits

To View Visit Requests:

  1. Go to Visits from sidebar
  2. View all visit requests with status filters:
    • Pending
    • Approved
    • Rejected
    • Completed
    • Deal Closed
    • Cancelled

To Approve/Reject Visit Request:

  1. Click on a visit request to view details
  2. Review:
    • Partner information
    • Property details
    • Client details
    • Requested date and time
  3. Click Approve or Reject
  4. Add notes if needed

To Mark Visit as Completed:

  1. Open an approved visit
  2. Click Mark as Completed or Mark as Deal Closed
  3. Add visit notes and feedback

5.4 Assigning Properties to Partners

  1. Go to Partners and click on partner name
  2. In Assigned Properties section, click Edit
  3. Select properties from the dropdown
  4. Click Save

7. Finance Manager Guide

As a Finance Manager, you manage commissions and payment tracking for partners.

7.1 Dashboard Overview

  • Total Commissions: All-time commission amount
  • Pending Amount: Commissions awaiting approval
  • Paid Amount: Completed payments
  • Due This Month: Upcoming payments

7.2 Creating Commission

  1. Go to Commissions from sidebar
  2. Click Add Commission
  3. Select the visit (must be marked as "Deal Closed")
  4. Enter commission details:
    • Deal Value
    • Commission Percentage (or override default rate)
    • Commission Amount (auto-calculated)
    • Notes/Remarks
  5. Click Create Commission
Note: Commissions can only be created for visits with status "Deal Closed". The system automatically calculates commission based on the partner's assigned rate.

7.3 Approving Commissions

  1. Go to Commissions and filter by "Pending"
  2. Click on a commission to view details
  3. Review:
    • Partner details
    • Property and deal value
    • Commission calculation
    • Visit notes
  4. Click Approve or Reject

7.4 Marking Commission as Paid

  1. Open an approved commission
  2. Click Mark as Paid
  3. Enter payment details:
    • Payment Date
    • Payment Reference/Transaction ID
    • Payment Mode
  4. Click Confirm Payment

7.5 Commission Status Flow

Status Description Next Action
Pending Commission created, awaiting approval Approve or Reject
Approved Commission approved, payment pending Mark as Paid
Paid Payment completed No further action
Rejected Commission rejected Can be re-created if needed

8. Channel Partner Guide

As a Channel Partner, you can apply to companies, book visits for your clients, and track your commissions.

8.1 Dashboard Overview

  • Active Partnerships: Companies you are partnered with
  • Total Visits: All your booked visits
  • Pending Visits: Visits awaiting approval
  • Total Earnings: Your commission earnings

8.2 Applying for Partnership

  1. Complete your profile and KYC verification
  2. Go to My Companies from sidebar
  3. Click Apply to Company
  4. Select the company you want to partner with
  5. Upload required documents:
    • PAN Card
    • Aadhaar Card
    • Business Registration (if applicable)
    • Any other documents requested
  6. Click Submit Application
Note: Your application will be reviewed by the company. You will be notified via email once approved.

8.3 Browsing Properties

  1. Go to Properties from sidebar
  2. View properties assigned to you by partnered companies
  3. Filter by:
    • Company
    • Property Type
    • Location
    • Status
  4. Click on property to view details, amenities, images, and documents

8.4 Booking a Visit

  1. Go to Visits from sidebar
  2. Click Book New Visit
  3. Select Company (from your active partnerships)
  4. Select Property (from company's assigned properties)
  5. Choose Visit Type:
    • Site Visit: Visit at the property location
    • Office Visit: Meeting at company office
    • Virtual Tour: Online video tour
  6. Enter the purpose of visit
  7. Optional: Check "I'm bringing a client with me" and fill client details:
    • Client Name
    • Client Phone Number
    • Client Email (Optional)
  8. For Office Visits: Select office location and available time slot
  9. Select Date (for Site/Virtual visits, also select time)
  10. Add any notes for the company
  11. Click Book Visit
Success: Your visit request has been submitted. You will be notified once the company approves or rejects your request.

8.5 Managing Your Visits

To View Your Visits:

  1. Go to Visits page
  2. View all your visits with status filters

To Cancel a Visit:

  1. Find the visit (must be in Pending or Approved status)
  2. Click Cancel
  3. Confirm cancellation

8.6 Tracking Commissions

  1. Go to Commissions from sidebar
  2. View all your commissions:
    • Pending commissions
    • Approved commissions awaiting payment
    • Paid commissions

8.7 Signing Agreements

  1. Go to Agreements from sidebar
  2. View agreements assigned to you
  3. Click on agreement to view details
  4. Read the agreement content carefully
  5. Click Sign Agreement
  6. Follow the e-signature process
  7. Confirm your signature

8.8 Communicating with Company

  1. Go to Chat from sidebar
  2. Select the company you want to message
  3. Type your message and send

10. Troubleshooting & FAQs

10.1 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I did not receive the verification email. What should I do?

A: Check your spam/junk folder. If still not found, go to login page and click "Resend Verification Email". Enter your registered email address.

Q: My account is locked. How do I unlock it?

A: After multiple failed login attempts, accounts are temporarily locked. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password.

Q: I cannot see any properties to book visits.

A: Properties are assigned by companies. Make sure:

  • You have an approved partnership with the company
  • The company has assigned properties to your partnership
  • Properties are marked as "Available"

Q: Why was my visit request rejected?

A: Common reasons include:

  • Property not available on requested date
  • Time slot already booked
  • Missing or incorrect client information
  • Property status changed to unavailable

Contact the company for specific reasons.

Q: How is my commission calculated?

A: Commission = Deal Value x Commission Rate. The commission rate is set by the company when approving your partnership. This can be viewed in your partnership details.

Q: Can I partner with multiple companies?

A: Yes, you can apply to and partner with multiple companies. Each partnership is managed separately.

Q: How do I update my KYC documents?

A: Go to Profile Settings, then KYC Documents section. Upload updated documents. The company will need to re-verify them.

Q: I accidentally cancelled a visit. Can I restore it?

A: No, cancelled visits cannot be restored. You will need to book a new visit request.

Q: Can I change the commission rate?

A: Commission rates are set by the company. If you need a different rate, please discuss with the company directly through chat or contact them.

10.2 Common Error Messages

Error Message Meaning Solution
"Invalid credentials" Email or password is incorrect Check your email and password. Use "Forgot Password" if needed.
"Email not verified" You have not clicked the verification link Check your email for verification link or request a new one.
"Session expired" Your login session has timed out Log in again. Your work should be auto-saved.
"Access denied" You do not have permission for this action Contact your company admin for access.
"No available slots" All time slots are booked for selected date Choose a different date or office location.
"File too large" Uploaded file exceeds size limit Reduce file size. Maximum allowed: 5MB for images, 10MB for documents.
